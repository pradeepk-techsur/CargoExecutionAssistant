`page.keyboard.press(...)` and `locator.press('Enter'/'Tab'/' ')` to activate

journey's PROGRESSION (button activations, link follows) — a `.click()` used

Tab reaches that same field elsewhere (don't rely solely on click-to-focus
for the SAME control the keyboard-completeness assertion is about).
