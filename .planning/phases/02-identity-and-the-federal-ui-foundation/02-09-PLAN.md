<objective>
Close the phase's two remaining criteria by making them artefacts rather than intentions: the
exclusion criterion becomes assertions in the architecture suite, and the accessibility
criterion becomes signed per-screen review records including an assistive-technology
walkthrough.

Purpose: scope pressure is the named project risk (PRD R-2), and TechArch §8.1 says why the
architecture suite is weighted so unusually — "in a product whose scope discipline is a stated
success metric (SM-14), absence must be verified, not remembered". Meanwhile accessibility has
**no CI gate and must never have one** (NFR-2, FR-2.25), which is exactly why §7.7's signed
review is mandatory rather than advisory: it is the only enforcement mechanism there is. A
screen without a signed record is not delivered.

Output: two new architecture specs, an extension to the existing absence spec, the USWDS
conformance register, and two signed review records — the last countersigned by the user at a
checkpoint, because a screen-reader walkthrough is the one thing here that a machine cannot do.
</objective>
